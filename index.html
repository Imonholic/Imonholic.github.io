🎙️ veylo - Make Your Microphone Sound Great

veylo is a free microphone studio for Windows. It removes background noise, keeps your voice at a steady level, lets you adjust the tone with a built-in equalizer, and remembers your settings as presets. Everything is processed locally on your own computer, so nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Open source. Offline audio processing. Development release.

📖 What veylo Does for You

Most people do not have a recording studio. They have a laptop, a headset, a fan in the corner, and a keyboard that clatters. That is usually enough to make a call, a recording, or a livestream sound worse than it needs to be.

veylo sits between your microphone and whatever app you are speaking into. It listens to your raw microphone signal and cleans it up before it reaches the other side. Hissing fans, hum from a charger, the rumble of traffic, and the sharp tick of a mechanical keyboard are reduced. Quiet sentences get lifted up so people do not have to strain to hear you. Harsh or muffled tone can be corrected with a few simple sliders.

You do not need to understand audio engineering to use it. You open the app, pick a preset that sounds close to what you want, and you are done. If you want to go further, every part of the chain can be adjusted by hand and saved under a name you choose.

✨ What You Can Do With veylo

  • Remove background noise. Noise suppression cuts steady sounds like fans, air conditioning, computer hum, and traffic so your voice comes through clearly.
  • Keep your volume even. Voice leveling turns loud laughter down and quiet asides up, so listeners never have to reach for their volume slider.
  • Shape your tone with EQ. The equalizer lets you add warmth to a thin voice, cut harshness from a bright microphone, or roll off low rumble that makes everything sound muddy.
  • Save and switch presets. Build a setup for calls, a setup for recording, and a setup for late-night gaming, then switch between them in a single click.
  • Work entirely offline. Your audio never leaves your PC. There is no account to create, no cloud service, and no upload step.
  • Stay open. veylo is open source, so anyone can inspect how the processing works and suggest improvements.

🎚️ Your First Five Minutes

The fastest way to get a good result is to start with a preset and then adjust only what bothers you.

  1. Pick your microphone. Choose your headset or USB microphone from the device list so veylo knows what to listen to.
  2. Turn on noise suppression. Speak normally while the fan or air conditioning runs. Slide the strength up until the background fades, then back off one step so your voice still sounds natural.
  3. Enable voice leveling. Read a sentence at normal volume, then whisper one. Both should end up roughly the same loudness when leveling is switched on.
  4. Adjust the equalizer gently. Small movements go a long way. If your voice sounds thin, nudge the lower-middle band up. If it sounds harsh or sharp, lower the upper band a little.
  5. Save it as a preset. Give it a name you will recognize, such as "Calls" or "Recording", so you can return to it instantly later.

🧩 Built-In Presets

veylo ships with starting points that already sound reasonable on ordinary hardware. Treat them as a first draft rather than a finished answer.

  • Calls and meetings. Strong noise suppression with light leveling, tuned so speech stays intelligible even on a poor connection.
  • Voice recording. Softer noise suppression that keeps more of the natural character of your voice, plus a gentle tone curve.
  • Streaming. Balanced suppression with a brighter EQ so you cut through game audio and music.
  • Quiet room. Minimal processing for when your environment is already clean and you want the most faithful sound.

💻 What Your PC Needs

  • A computer running Windows 10 or Windows 11.
  • Any microphone Windows can already see, such as a headset, a webcam microphone, or a USB microphone.
  • Roughly 200 MB of free disk space for the application and its processing models.
  • 4 GB of memory or more. Audio processing is light, but a comfortable amount of memory keeps everything smooth.
  • No internet connection is required to use veylo after you have downloaded it.

🔒 Privacy: Your Voice Stays on Your PC

All noise suppression, leveling, and equalizer work happens on your own machine. Nothing is sent to a server. There is no account, no sign-in, no telemetry about what you say, and no cloud processing tier.

This matters for anyone taking private calls, recording sensitive notes, or working somewhere with a slow or unreliable connection. veylo behaves the same way on a plane as it does on fibre.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

My microphone does not appear in the list

Check that the microphone works in Windows itself by opening Sound settings and watching the input level meter while you speak. If Windows cannot see it, close veylo, plug the microphone back in, and open veylo again.

My voice sounds robotic or watery

Noise suppression is working too hard. Reduce its strength by one or two steps. Very aggressive settings can start treating parts of speech as noise.

People say I sound distant

Move the microphone closer, then lower the input volume slightly. A close microphone with modest processing always beats a distant one with heavy processing.

The app closes unexpectedly

This is a development release, so rough edges are expected. Download the newest release from the link above, since the issue may already be fixed.

I want to start over

Switch noise suppression and leveling off, set every equalizer band back to the middle, and begin again with a preset.
